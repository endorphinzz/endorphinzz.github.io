About Me 关于我

Hello, I am Yuhang Wang, a PhD student at Tsinghua University Shenzhen International Graduate School (SIGS), majoring in Computer Science and Technology. Currently, the areas I am exploring include: Large Language Models Safety and LLM4 IoT Security. I received my Bachelor's degree (2022–2026) in Software Engineering from Southwest University.

My research interests mainly include:

  • Large Language Models Safety: LLMs jailbreak attacks and defenses; LLM4 IoT Security.
  • Multimodal LLMs: Code Generation.
