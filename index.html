About Me 关于我

I am an incoming PhD student at Tsinghua University Shenzhen International Graduate School (SIGS), majoring in Computer Science and Technology. Currently, the areas I am exploring include: Large Language Models Safety and LLM4 IoT_security. I will soon receive my Bachelor's degree (2022–2026) in Software Engineering from Southwest University.

My research interests mainly include:

  • Large Language Models Safety: LLMs jailbreak attacks and defenses.
  • Multimodal LLMs: Code Generation.
  • Data Mining: User Behavior Modeling, Smart Homes, 3D Scene Understanding.

我即将进入清华大学深圳国际研究生院攻读计算机科学与技术博士学位。目前我正在探索的研究方向包括：大语言模型安全和LLM4 IoT安全。 我将于2026年获得西南大学软件工程学士学位。

我的研究兴趣主要包括：

  • 大语言模型安全：LLM越狱攻击与防御。
  • 多模态大语言模型：代码生成。
  • 数据挖掘：用户行为建模、智能家居、3D场景理解。
